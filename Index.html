How to Calculate GPA

To calculate your GPA, sum your grades and divide by the number of subjects.

Example: (14 + 12 + 16) ÷ 3 = 14 GPA

Tips to Improve Your GPA

  • Review lessons regularly
  • Organize your study schedule
  • Get enough sleep and eat healthy
  • Ask teachers or friends if you don’t understand
